Project Meridian — Product Category Revenue
EXPLAIN BEFORE Physical Design

Query:
SELECT
    p.category,
    SUM(o.quantity * o.unit_price - o.discount_amount) AS revenue
FROM meridian.orders o
JOIN meridian.products p
  ON p.product_id = o.product_id
GROUP BY p.category
ORDER BY revenue DESC;

Observed plan:
- Estimated cost: approximately 750
- Hash join between orders and products
- Hash/group-by execution
- Local resegmentation was required
- orders_super and products_super were used
- Orders estimated at 13 rows
- Products estimated at 5 rows
- Join estimated at 11 rows
- Final result estimated at 4 rows
- SIP runtime filtering was present

Interpretation:
Before the custom physical design, the query used a hash join and
required local resegmentation rather than using projections explicitly
designed around the product_id join key.
