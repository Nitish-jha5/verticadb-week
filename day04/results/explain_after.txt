Project Meridian — Product Category Revenue
EXPLAIN AFTER Physical Design

Query:
SELECT
    p.category,
    SUM(o.quantity * o.unit_price - o.discount_amount) AS revenue
FROM meridian.orders o
JOIN meridian.products p
  ON p.product_id = o.product_id
GROUP BY p.category
ORDER BY revenue DESC;

Observed plan:
- Estimated cost: approximately 734
- Merge join between orders_join and products_join
- Both projections are designed around product_id
- orders_join is segmented by HASH(product_id)
- products_join is segmented by HASH(product_id)
- Inputs were presorted for the merge join
- orders_join estimated at 13 rows
- products_join estimated at 5 rows
- Join estimated at 11 rows
- Final result estimated at 4 rows
- SIP merge-join runtime filtering was present

Interpretation:
After the physical design, the product-category query used the
purpose-built orders_join and products_join projections and changed
from the previous hash-join/resegmentation plan to a merge join
using product_id-aligned projections.

The estimated cost decreased from approximately 750 to approximately
734. The plan-shape improvement is the primary evidence of the
physical-design change.

The dataset contains only 13 orders and 5 products, so execution-time
measurements are too small/noisy to claim a reliable wall-clock
improvement.
